This document presents example results of the analysis of acute cardiovascular events (MI and stroke) with GP diagnosed (suspected) UTI as exposure. 
Suspected UTIs with positive culture are compared to suspected UTIs with no microbiological evidence of UTI.

This document presents results for MI, from one imputed dataset. Final results were obtained by pooling these results with results from the other imputed datsets.
Number of events and total n have been rounded to the nearest ten.
